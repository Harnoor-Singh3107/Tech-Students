Cafe Ordering Site – Meal Magic
Welcome to Meal Magic, your go-to destination for delicious, hassle-free food ordering and delivery. This project is a web-based cafe ordering platform designed to provide users with a seamless dining and ordering experience, whether for takeout, delivery, or table reservation.

Features
Browse Menu: Explore a diverse menu featuring local and international cuisines, including appetizers, main courses, desserts, and beverages.

Add to Cart: Users can add items to their cart, view order summaries, and adjust quantities before checkout.

Order Summary: Real-time calculation of subtotal, delivery fee, and total order amount.

Multiple Payment Options: Secure payment via credit card, debit card, PayPal, cash on delivery, or QR code scan.

Reservation System: Book a table for special occasions and receive confirmation of your reservation.

Responsive Design: Optimized for both desktop and mobile devices.

Customer Support: Fast delivery, secure payment, and top-notch customer service

How to Use
Browse the Menu: Navigate to the menu page and select your favorite dishes.

Add to Cart: Click to add items to your cart. Adjust quantities as needed.

Checkout: Go to the cart, review your order, and proceed to payment. Choose your preferred payment method or scan the QR code for instant payment.

Reservation (Optional): Book a table for dine-in via the reservation page.

Enjoy Your Meal: Sit back and relax while your order is prepared and delivered!

Customization
Menu: Update Menu.html to add or modify food items and prices.

Styling: Modify style-cart.css and style-about.css for custom branding and layout.

Payment Methods: Extend script.js to integrate with real payment gateways as needed.
